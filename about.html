About Convergence Master

Convergence Master is an educational cybersecurity awareness platform built by students from Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India. Our mission is to reduce cyber fraud in India.

According to UP Cyber Crime Portal, UPI fraud increased by 300% in 2024. Most victims are students, shopkeepers, and elderly who don't know how to identify fake UPI IDs, QR codes, and loan apps.

Our Mission - Digital India Safe India

We provide 20 Interactive Labs that simulate real frauds: UPI ID Validator, QR Code Scam Detector, OTP Interception Demo, WhatsApp Forward Checker, Instagram Fake Profile, Call Spoofing, WiFi Evil Twin, Password Strength, Email Header Analyzer, AI Deepfake Detector, Loan App Fraud, Job Scam Detector.

All labs run locally in browser, no data is collected, no external APIs. 100% safe for students.

Why We Built This?

For college project + social good. We promote official helpline 1930 (24x7) and cybercrime.gov.in. If you are scammed, report within 1 hour - money can be recovered via lien.
