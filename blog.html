MOST READ - INDIA #1 FRAUD

1. How to Identify Fake UPI ID? (UPI Scam Explained)

What is Fake UPI? Scammers create UPI IDs like rbi-official@okaxis, gov-refund@ybl, electricity-bill@paytm. They look official but are personal accounts.

🔴 How to Identify?

  • RBI, GOV, OFFICIAL words in UPI = 99% fake. RBI never has UPI ID.
  • Personal name @okaxis cannot be a government department.
  • Refund never comes via UPI. Real refund comes to bank account.
  • Check @ part: okaxis, ybl, paytm are payment apps, not government.

📚 Real Case from Delhi:

500 people scammed - 30 Lakhs lost. Scammer used rbi-refund@okaxis. Sent SMS: "Your account will be blocked, refund your money via UPI". People sent money, never got back.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe?

  • Never pay to UPI with rbi, gov, official in name.
  • Always verify with official email @rbi.org.in
  • Ask for GST bill if it's a shop.
  • Test with 1 Rupee first for new UPI.
  • If scammed, call 1930 within 1 hour.
TRENDING IN MARKETS

2. QR Code Scam - Pay vs Collect Explained

There are 2 types of QR: PAY QR (you pay to shop) and COLLECT QR (shop collects from you - money debited FROM you).

🔴 Scam Trick:

  • Scammer pastes his QR over real shop QR.
  • He sends you COLLECT QR on WhatsApp and says "Scan to get refund". If you scan, money goes FROM your account.
  • QR with amount fixed like upi://pay?pa=scam@okaxis&am=5000 is COLLECT type.

🛡️ Safe QR Check:

✅ Real shop QR is laminated, only has pa (UPI) and pn (name), NO amount. ❌ Fake QR has am=amount. Before scanning, check if 2 QRs pasted. If screen shows "Collect Request", CANCEL immediately.

LOAN APP FRAUD

3. Loan App Fraud - 28 Permissions Trap

Fake loan apps on Play Store ask 28 permissions. Real loan app needs only 5.

🔴 Dangerous Permissions:

  • SMS Read - To steal OTP and bank OTPs
  • Contacts Read - To blackmail your family if you don't pay
  • Camera, Location, Storage - For harassment

Real Case: User took 2000 loan, app accessed contacts and sent nude morphed photos to family, demanded 50,000. Report such apps on cybercrime.gov.in

🛡️ Safety:

Check rating < 4.0, check permissions before install, never give SMS permission, check RBI registered NBFC list.

JOB SCAM 2024

4. Job Scam - Telegram Task Fraud (5000/day)

You get message: "Work from home, Rs 5000/day, just do Telegram tasks". This is 96% scam.

🔴 How it Works:

Day 1: Give small tasks, pay 100 Rs. Day 2: Ask you to pay 500 to get bigger task. Day 3: Ask 5000. You pay, they block you. No company GST, no interview, only Telegram ID.

🛡️ Real Job Never:

  • Asks money first
  • Works only on Telegram
  • Offers 5000/day for liking videos
